## How to Use with Google Patents

1. Go to [patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com)
2. In the search bar, enter the CPC code: `CPC:G06N3/00`
3. Combine with keywords: `CPC:G06N3/00 "edge inference" "on-device"`
4. Filter by: **Grant Status** → Granted | **Date** → 2004–2025

For Espacenet (EPO):
1. Go to [worldwide.espacenet.com](https://worldwide.espacenet.com)
2. Use **Smart Search**, enter: `cl = G06N3/00 AND ti = "neural network pruning"`
