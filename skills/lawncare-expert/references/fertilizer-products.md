## Application Rate Calculator

### Formula
```
Product needed (lbs) = (Desired N per 1000 sq ft × Lawn size in thousands) ÷ (N% as decimal)

Example: 
  Want: 1 lb N/1000 sq ft
  Lawn: 5,000 sq ft
  Product: 24-0-12

  (1 × 5) ÷ 0.24 = 20.83 lbs of product needed
  50 lb bag covers: 50 × 0.24 = 12 lbs N total ÷ 1 = 12,000 sq ft coverage
```

## Spreader Settings Guidance

> ⚠️ Spreader settings vary by model and product. ALWAYS calibrate with a known area first.

### General Approach
1. Set spreader to manufacturer's recommended setting
2. Measure a 100 sq ft test strip
3. Weigh product collected
4. Adjust up/down to hit target rate

### Common Spreader Types
- **Rotary (broadcast)** — Covers wide swath, overlap passes 30%
- **Drop spreader** — Precise edges, overlap wheels. Good for near beds/driveways.
- **Handheld** — Small areas and spot treatment only
